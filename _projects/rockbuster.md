## Project Overview

Rockbuster Stealth LLC is a fictional movie rental company preparing to launch a new streaming video service.

The goal of this project was to analyze Rockbuster's customer and payment data to determine where the company should initially focus its streaming strategy.

The analysis examined customer distribution, geographic markets, and high-value customers to identify markets with the greatest potential for the new service.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on three primary questions:

1. Where should Rockbuster initially focus its new streaming service?
2. Which countries and cities contribute the most revenue?
3. Where are Rockbuster's highest-value customers located?

## Data

The analysis used Rockbuster's internal customer data.

The available data included:

- Film inventory
- Customer information
- Payment information

Some overall characteristics of the rental business included an average rental duration of 4.9 days, an average rental rate of $2.98, and an average film length of 115 minutes.

## Tools & Methods

SQL was used to explore and analyze the underlying relational data.

The analysis focused on:

- Customer counts by country
- Customer counts by city
- Payment totals by customer
- Geographic comparison of customer markets
- Identification of high-volume and high-value markets

The results were then communicated through Tableau visualizations to make geographic and customer patterns easier to identify.

## Analysis

### Customer Distribution by Country

India had the highest number of Rockbuster customers, followed by China and the United States.

The top customer markets included:

1. India — 35,940 customers
2. China — 31,747 customers
3. United States — 20,965 customers
4. Japan — 18,569 customers
5. Mexico — 17,970 customers

The customer base was distributed across multiple international markets, indicating that Rockbuster's streaming strategy would need to account for different geographic and linguistic markets.

### Customer Distribution by City

The city-level analysis revealed an especially strong presence in India.

Five of the ten cities with the highest customer counts were located in India.

This concentration suggests that Indian cities could provide an important starting point for Rockbuster's streaming launch strategy.

### High-Value Customers

The analysis also examined customers based on their total payments.

Three of the five highest-paying customers were located in India.

This provides additional evidence that India represents an important market—not only because of its large customer base, but also because of the value generated by individual customers.

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several important patterns:

- India has the largest customer population among the countries analyzed.
- Five of the ten cities with the highest customer counts are in India.
- Three of the five highest-paying customers are located in India.
- Rockbuster's customers are distributed across multiple countries and languages.
- High customer volume and high customer value provide useful indicators for prioritizing potential streaming markets.

## Recommendations

### 1. Begin with High-Volume, High-Value Cities

Rockbuster should initially focus its streaming launch on cities that combine a large customer base with strong customer value.

### 2. Target Marketing Toward High-Customer-Count Regions

Marketing resources should be concentrated in regions where Rockbuster already has a substantial customer base.

Existing customers provide a potential audience for converting from the rental business to the new streaming service.

### 3. Prioritize India as a Key Market

India stands out across multiple measures.

It has the highest overall customer count, five of the ten cities with the highest customer counts, and three of the five highest-paying customers.

These findings make India an especially strong candidate for early investment and marketing.

### 4. Consider Emerging Cities for Future Expansion

After establishing the service in high-volume markets, Rockbuster could expand into emerging cities with potential for future growth.

### 5. Account for International Markets

Rockbuster's customer base spans countries with multiple languages, including English, Chinese, Spanish, and Japanese.

The streaming strategy should therefore account for the international nature of the customer base when developing marketing and customer communications.

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that Rockbuster should use its existing customer base to guide the initial launch of its streaming service.

India represents the clearest priority market based on customer volume, city concentration, and high-value customers.

More broadly, the analysis demonstrates how customer and payment data can be used to identify priority geographic markets and support business expansion decisions.

## Lessons Learned

This project strengthened my ability to use SQL to explore relational business data and translate analytical results into business recommendations.

It also reinforced the importance of examining data at multiple levels—country, city, and individual customer—to identify patterns that may not be apparent from a single metric.

Finally, Tableau provided a useful way to communicate the geographic and customer patterns identified through the analysis.
