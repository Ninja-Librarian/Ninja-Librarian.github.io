## Project Overview

Instacart is an online grocery shopping platform with a large and diverse customer base.

The goal of this project was to explore purchasing behavior and customer characteristics to identify shopping patterns and develop recommendations for targeted marketing and increased customer engagement.

The analysis examined when customers shop, which departments perform best, and how customer demographics vary across regions.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on several key questions:

1. What are the busiest days and hours for Instacart orders?
2. Which departments are most popular in different regions?
3. What do the customer demographics look like across age and income groups?
4. How do customer demographics compare across regions?
5. How can these findings be used to support targeted marketing?

## Data

The analysis used several categories of Instacart data:

- Customer demographics
- Order history
- Product information
- Departments and product categories

The data provided information about customer characteristics, purchasing activity, and the products and departments included in orders.

## Tools & Methods

Python was used to clean, transform, explore, and visualize the data.

- Exploratory data analysis
- Data visualization

Jupyter Notebook was used as the primary analysis environment, with Pandas used for data manipulation and analysis.

## Analysis

### Shopping Patterns

The analysis found that Sunday and Saturday were the busiest shopping days, while Tuesday and Wednesday were the least busy.

Most orders were placed between 10 AM and 3 PM.

These patterns provide useful information for determining when customers are most active and when marketing efforts may have the greatest opportunity to reach them.

### Department Performance

Produce, dairy/eggs, and beverages were the three highest-performing departments.

The West and South regions had the highest number of purchases.

Produce also had the highest sales compared with the other departments analyzed.

These findings suggest that inventory planning should account for regional differences in purchasing behavior, particularly in the West and South.

### Customer Demographics

Customers under the age of 35 represented the largest age group overall.

Most customers in this age group fell into either the under $60K or $60K–$94K income categories.

Customers between ages 35 and 50 showed a more even distribution across income groups.

Examining age and income together provided a more detailed view of the customer base than looking at either demographic independently.

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several important patterns:

- Saturday and Sunday were the busiest shopping days.
- Tuesday and Wednesday were the least busy shopping days.
- Most orders occurred between 10 AM and 3 PM.
- Produce, dairy/eggs, and beverages were the top-performing departments.
- The West and South regions had the highest number of purchases.
- Produce had the highest sales among the departments analyzed.
- Customers under 35 represented the largest age group.
- Customers ages 35–50 were more evenly distributed across income groups.

## Recommendations

### 1. Adjust Advertising Around Customer Activity

The analysis recommended running advertisements on Tuesday and Wednesday during:

- 6 AM–8 AM
- 5 PM–10 PM

Advertising should receive particular attention around 5 PM and 7 PM, with 12 PM representing a secondary opportunity.

### 2. Prioritize Inventory in High-Demand Regions

The West and South regions should maintain strong inventory levels for:

- Produce
- Dairy/eggs
- Beverages
- Snacks

This would help ensure that inventory is available in regions with higher purchasing activity.

### 3. Target High-Income Seniors

Marketing campaigns should include high-income senior customers across regions.

The demographic analysis suggests that this group represents an opportunity for targeted marketing rather than relying on a single strategy for the entire customer base.

### 4. Consider Family-Oriented Promotions

Families may respond well to bulk deals, particularly in the South and West regions.

This provides an opportunity to align promotional strategies with both customer demographics and regional purchasing behavior.

## Conclusion

The Instacart analysis demonstrates how customer demographics, purchasing behavior, product categories, and regional patterns can be combined to support targeted marketing and operational decisions.

The results show that customer activity varies by day, time, region, and demographic group. Using these patterns to guide advertising, inventory planning, and promotions can help create a more targeted approach to customer engagement.
