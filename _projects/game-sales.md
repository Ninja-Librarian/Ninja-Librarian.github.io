## Project Overview

This project analyzed historical video game sales data to identify changes in regional markets, game popularity, and the performance of major publishers.

The analysis examined sales across three major geographic markets:

- North America
- Europe
- Japan

The goal was to determine whether video game sales had remained relatively stable across regions and to identify the publishers performing most strongly in each market.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on three primary questions:

1. Who are the key competitors in different markets?
2. How have video game sales changed over time?
3. How do sales trends differ between geographic regions?

## Data

The project used historical video game sales data covering **1980–2016**.

The dataset was sourced from **VGChartz**, a video game sales and market data resource.

### Data Source

[VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/)

## Tools & Methods

The analysis was completed using **Microsoft Excel**.

Key techniques included:

- Pivot tables
- Data organization and filtering
- Regional sales comparisons
- Trend analysis
- Market comparisons
- Data visualization

## Analysis

### Regional Markets

North America, Europe, and Japan were examined separately to identify differences in sales patterns and market performance.

The analysis showed that regional sales did **not** remain stable over time.

Europe emerged as an increasingly important market, while North American sales had begun to decline from their previous position as the leading market. Japan experienced a decline beginning in the late 1990s and had not returned to its earlier sales levels.

### Sales Trends Over Time

The historical data showed meaningful changes in the relative performance of the major markets.

Key observations included:

- **Europe** showed an upward trend and represented an important area of potential growth.
- **North America** remained the largest market but had experienced a recent downturn.
- **Japan** experienced a significant decline beginning in the late 1990s and had not recovered to previous levels.

These findings challenged the original assumption that sales had remained consistent across geographic regions.

### Publisher Competition

The project also examined the top publishers across the major markets using data from **1983–2016**.

The analysis identified several leading competitors:

| Publisher | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Nintendo | Led the North American, European, and Japanese markets |
| Electronic Arts | Ranked second in North America, Europe, and Other markets |
| Activision | Ranked among the leading publishers in North America and Europe |
| Sony | More competitive in the Japanese and Other markets |

Nintendo was the strongest overall competitor across the three primary markets analyzed.

## Key Findings

### 1. Regional markets are changing

The analysis found that the assumption of stable regional sales was inaccurate. Market performance changed considerably over time.

### 2. Europe represents a growth opportunity

Europe showed an upward sales trend and should be monitored as an increasingly important market.

### 3. North America remains important

Although North American sales were slowing, the region remained the largest market in the analysis.

### 4. Japan followed a different trajectory

Japan experienced a downturn beginning in the late 1990s and had not recovered to its earlier sales levels.

### 5. A small group of publishers dominated the market

Nintendo, Electronic Arts, and Activision emerged as the three leading competitors identified in the analysis.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, a video game company evaluating these markets should:

- Monitor European sales closely because of its upward trend.
- Continue treating North America as a major market while accounting for its recent slowdown.
- Approach Japan differently because its historical sales pattern differs from North America and Europe.
- Track the strategies and performance of leading publishers, particularly Nintendo, Electronic Arts, and Activision.
- Avoid assuming that historical regional performance will remain constant over time.

## Conclusion

This analysis demonstrated how historical sales data can challenge assumptions about market performance.

The most important finding was that **video game sales patterns differed significantly by region and changed over time**. Europe showed growth potential, North America remained the largest market despite a recent downturn, and Japan followed a substantially different trajectory.

The analysis also identified Nintendo as the strongest competitor across the three primary markets, with Electronic Arts and Activision among the other leading publishers.
