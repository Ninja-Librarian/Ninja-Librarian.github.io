# Game Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

An analysis of historical video game sales data to identify trends in game popularity, regional markets, and leading competitors.

## Business Questions

- Who are the key competitors in different markets?
- How have video game sales changed over time?
- How do sales trends differ between geographic regions?

## Data

The analysis used historical video game sales data covering 1980–2016.

**Source:** [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/)

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft PowerPoint
- Pivot tables
- Data visualization

## Analysis

The analysis examined video game sales across North America, Europe, and Japan, with particular attention to changes in regional market share and the performance of major publishers.

## Key Findings

- Europe had the highest physical game sales in the current market.
- European sales showed an upward trend.
- North America had historically been the largest market but was experiencing a downturn.
- Japan experienced a decline beginning in the late 1990s and had not recovered.
- Nintendo was the leading competitor across the North American, European, and Japanese markets.
- Electronic Arts and Activision were also major competitors.

## Conclusions

The analysis did not support the assumption that video game sales had remained stable across regions.

Regional market shares changed over time, with Europe showing growth potential while North America remained the largest but slower market.
